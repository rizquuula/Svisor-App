**Svisor** is an Android app for drilling supervisors. It helps field teams record daily drilling data, prepare monthly reports, and share field pictures. Svisor stores all report data in the team's own Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Svisor is developed by **M Razif Rizqullah** under **Lyralabs**.

[Get Svisor on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lyralabs.svisor) · [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

---

## Features

- **Daily data entry**: record daily drilling activity with activity codes, guidelines, and input validation.
- **Monthly report**: build the monthly drilling report from the daily data, with carry-over from the previous month.
- **Picture reporting**: take or choose field photos and attach them to reports.
- **Google Drive folders**: open the report folders and templates that your team keeps in Google Drive.
- **Guidelines**: read the in-app guidelines for each data field.
- **English and Indonesian**: use the app in either language.

## How Svisor uses your Google data

You sign in to Svisor with your Google account. Svisor then asks for access to these Google services:

| Access | Why Svisor needs it |
|---|---|
| Your email address and profile | To sign you in and show your account in the app. |
| Google Sheets | To read and write the daily and monthly drilling report spreadsheets. |
| Google Drive | To find the report folders and templates in your Drive, create report files, and save field photos. |

Svisor sends this data only between your device and Google's services. The developer does not run servers that store your Google data. Svisor does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Svisor's use of information from Google APIs complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Svisor's access at any time on the [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) page.
